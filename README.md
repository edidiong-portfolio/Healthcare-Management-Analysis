## Problem Solved

The dashboard provides one simple view of **patient activity, treatment costs, insurance coverage, and location performance**.

It helps identify where healthcare costs are high and where insurance coverage may need improvement.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| **Total Treatment Cost** | **$6.88M** |
| **Patients Without Insurance** | **34%** |
| **Highest-Cost Location** | **Los Angeles** |
| **Highest-Cost Month** | **January** |

---

## Key Insights

* Total treatment cost reached **$6.88M**.
* **Los Angeles** had the highest treatment cost.
* **34% of patients** had no insurance.
* Treatment costs were fairly balanced across treatment types.
* **January** recorded the highest monthly cost.

---

## Recommendations

1. Improve insurance coverage.
2. Review high healthcare costs in Los Angeles.
3. Monitor expensive treatments.
4. Plan resources around high-cost periods.
5. Improve support for uninsured patients.

---

## Business Outcome

The dashboard helps identify:

* Cost drivers
* Insurance coverage gaps
* High-cost locations
* Treatment spending trends
* Resource planning needs

This supports better cost control and healthcare resource planning.
